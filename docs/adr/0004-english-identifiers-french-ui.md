# English identifiers, French UI

A French-only glossary leaked into tables, functions, test titles and commits.
`/implement` copies whatever `CONTEXT.md` writes, and `CONTEXT.md` (`## Language`)
is the single statement of the language rule. This ADR records why the split
matters rather than restating it: the glossary is the vocabulary — Payback is
the writing, not Settlement or Payment — Payment collides with "Léa paid for
pizza" (an Expense).
